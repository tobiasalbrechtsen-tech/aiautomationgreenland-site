Questions about the campaign

If you cannot find the answer, write to us. We reply within one working day.

What does it cost if we stop after the pilot?

Nothing. If you let us know by the last day of month 3, you pay neither setup nor operation. The solution is switched off, and your data is returned or deleted.

Why are there only 3 places?

We build each solution thoroughly and follow it closely during the pilot. We can only do that for three businesses at a time.

Is there a lock-in?

If you continue after the pilot, the agreement runs for 12 months, because the setup is paid over that period. If you would rather avoid a lock-in, you can choose 30% off the setup and pay it right away instead.

What if we are in Greenland?

Exactly the same applies. Customers in Greenland pay no Danish VAT, and everything happens online.

When do we need to decide?

By 31 January 2027 at the latest, or when the three places are taken.
