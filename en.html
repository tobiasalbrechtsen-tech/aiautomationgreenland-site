    Frequently asked questions

    Can’t find your answer? Write to us. We reply within one business day.

    How much does a chatbot cost?

    A chatbot starts at DKK 6,900 for setup and DKK 595 per month for operation and AI usage. With calendar booking, the price is DKK 12,900 and DKK 995 per month. Tourism businesses can get a lower seasonal price in the winter months.

    Where is our data stored?

    We sign a data processing agreement with you and keep data in the EU where possible. You own your data and conversations.

    Do we need to replace our systems?

    No. We build on what you already use, such as your website, calendar, Microsoft 365 or Google.

    What if the chatbot can’t answer?

    It passes the question on to you with the customer’s contact details, so you can reply personally.

    Can the chatbot speak several languages?

    Yes. It can answer in English, Danish and other languages, so tourists and international customers get answers too.
